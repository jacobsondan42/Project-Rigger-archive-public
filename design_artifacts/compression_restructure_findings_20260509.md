## The restructure

### Universal property

Every compression's error term is composed of parts immaterial to the parties tracking the compression. What shows up in the error term, across both productive and failure outcomes, is always that composition. The phrase **immaterial-to-both** names this composition: the dropped dimensions weren't tracked by any party transacting the compression because they didn't show up in any party's accounting of what mattered.

This is the universal property because it holds across every compression operation examined in this session:

- *Practice-as-compiler.* Deliberation steps not encountered during practice are immaterial-to-both at the compile stage; if encountered later, they're the error.
- *Selection-as-compiler.* Environmental dimensions not imposing selection pressure are immaterial-to-both during selection; if the environment shifts and they become operative, they're the error term — evolutionary mismatch is the canonical case.
- *Materiality-mismatch (already named under principle #1).* Compounding-pay, hidden selection criteria, register mismatch — error term is composed of dimensions material to a known counterparty but immaterial to the compressing party.

The directionality-and-surfacing operationalization of constitutional principle #1 takes a tighter form under this frame: "name the error term and probe which parties it might become material to, across the relevant horizon." Less abstract than "name the compression direction"; more actionable.

### Mechanism children

Different ways compression operates as an operation. Children of compression in the strict architectural sense — they are *kinds* of compression rather than *instances*, properties, or outcomes:

1. **Practice-as-compiler.** Within-individual compression: deliberate System 2 process compiled into automatic System 1 reflex through repetition. Fast (within a lifetime). Requires sustained engagement. Examples: AAR repeated until compare-desire-to-outcome becomes intuitive; records-keeping discipline repeated until provenance-and-source-bias capture becomes reflexive; ritual-and-liturgy in religious practice compiling eternal-horizon frame into System 1 background. The Kahnemanian System 1 / System 2 dual-process architecture is the cognitive substrate; the framework-level abstraction is that deliberate slow processes compile into automatic fast surfaces through repetition. The compiled surface drops the deliberation steps; what was never encountered during practice is immaterial-to-both at compile time.

2. **Selection-as-compiler.** Population-level compression: environment rendered into trait/skill fitness through evolutionary or economic pressure. Slow (across generations or careers). Operates without conscious effort by individuals; the population-scale process does the compression via differential survival/reproduction/selection. Examples: cognitive adaptations to ancestral environments; market selection of firms by profitability; institutional culture selection of practices by survival of the institutional vehicle. The compiled trait drops the environmental dimensions that didn't impose selection pressure; what wasn't pressured-on is immaterial-to-both at selection time.

Both children share the universal property: the compiler drops dimensions that weren't repeatedly-encountered (practice) or weren't selection-pressured-upon (selection). When dropped dimensions become material later, that's the error term activating.

The children list is non-exhaustive. Plausible candidates not yet examined in this session:

- *Language-as-compiler.* Terminology compresses concepts; what's left out becomes the error when the term is applied to edge cases.
- *Institution-as-compiler.* Roles, processes, and procedures compress complex coordination problems; what's not codified is immaterial-to-both until it becomes material.
- *Culture-as-compiler.* Shared norms compress decision space; what's not normed is immaterial-to-both within the culture.

These are flagged as candidates pending a scoped enumeration pass.

### Outcome subsets

Productive vs. failure are downstream observations, not structural children. They're evaluated against whether the immaterial-to-both parts turn out to be material to some party with standing.

- **Productive.** The immaterial-to-both parts remain immaterial across the horizon over which the compression is consumed. The compression does its work without the error term mattering. The classification is always provisional — "productive within the horizon and parties evaluated so far"; activation conditions can shift.
- **Failure.** The immaterial-to-both parts turn out to be material to some party — either tracked at compression-time but their materiality was misjudged, or untracked at compression-time and surfaced later.

Materiality-mismatch (already named under principle #1) is the failure-outcome case where the second party is a known counterparty (compounding-pay's labor side; hidden-criteria's candidate; register-mismatch's wider audience). Other failure cases stay open as taxonomy work — sibling failure modes have not been enumerated and probably want their own pass.

### Fractality as sibling meta-principle

Fractality is the property where the same operation recurs at multiple scales. The architecture pass found compression to be flat-fractal in shape; that means fractality is a *property of compression*, not a *kind of compression*. Making fractality a child of compression would invert the architectural relationship.

Fractality is a sibling meta-principle that applies to compression and possibly to other meta-principles. Other candidate meta-principles (trust-as-rate-limiter, capture-not-evaluate) are themselves potentially fractal in shape; the property might apply broadly across the framework's meta-level. The architecture pass already treats fractality this way ("fractal application as structural property of framework principles" rather than "kinds of compression"); this artifact preserves that treatment.

## Connection to existing records

### Operationalized-minimum vs core-insight gap (architecture pass finding)

The materiality-mismatch sub-pattern under principle #1 was reading the phenomenon at half-resolution. Materiality-mismatch is the **operationalized minimum** — the specific case where the error term hits a known counterparty. The **core insight** is the universal property of immaterial-to-both error-term composition, which encompasses materiality-mismatch as one case but generalizes beyond it.

This is a clean instance of the operationalized-minimum/core-insight gap from the architecture pass: a real failure mode was named (materiality-mismatch) but the more general operation it sits within (universal property of error-term composition) wasn't surfaced. The restructure pulls the core insight through.

A second-order observation: the operationalized-minimum/core-insight gap pattern itself appears to apply *recursively*. The pattern is not a one-time surfacing; it can surface within previously-named sub-patterns. The discipline implication is that when a sub-pattern is named, it's worth asking whether the named pattern is the core insight or whether it's the operationalized minimum of something more general. This recursion is candidate for codification as its own framework-level discipline.

### Carved-out passes (architecture pass deliverable)

Two passes carved out from the architecture pass deliverable get sharper articulation under the restructure:

- **Bayesian network of compression-conditioning inputs.** The pass is mapping how a compression decides what gets tracked versus relegated to immaterial-to-both. Under the restructure, the conditioning network *is* the materiality-classification mechanism. The pass's deliverable should articulate what it's classifying (materiality at compression-time) and against what reference (which parties' standing, across what horizon). The "across the relevant horizon" framing folds in the temporal dimension that the temporal-drift pass also touches.
- **Temporal/environmental drift.** The pass is studying the case where parts correctly immaterial-to-both at compression-time become material later as environments shift. Under the restructure, this is a specific subset of the failure outcome category — error term that sits dormant until conditions activate it. The pass's scope can sharpen around this specific sub-case.

Both passes were already named in the architecture pass findings; the restructure provides them a tighter common surface to operate against.

### System-level fractal meta-finding (architecture pass)

The system-level fractal meta-finding (principles + agent + records + utility-function carving compose as a coherent fractal system) is consistent with the restructure. The framework's operational architecture includes compression-as-meta operating at every layer, with fractality applying to that operation across scales. The two findings are complementary rather than competing.

## Open questions

1. **Mechanism children completeness.** The list above (practice-as-compiler, selection-as-compiler) plus candidate extensions (language, institution, culture) — is it exhaustive at the mechanism-shape level, or are there mechanisms not yet considered? Worth a scoped pass.

2. **Failure-mode taxonomy.** Materiality-mismatch is named. Sibling cases (temporal mismatch when error material to future parties; externality mismatch when error material to parties outside the transaction frame; latent mismatch when materiality activates later under shifted conditions) were sketched in this session but not validated. Worth a scoped pass.

3. **Fractality and compression interaction.** Each mechanism child appears to be flat-fractal in its own right (practice-as-compiler operates at micro/meso/macro scales of practice; selection-as-compiler operates at organism/firm/institution scales). The restructure implies a stack: compression at the mechanism level, fractality applying to that mechanism across scales, with both meta-principles composing. Worth surfacing whether this stack-architecture has additional structural implications.

4. **Other meta-principles.** This restructure addresses compression specifically. Trust-as-rate-limiter, capture-not-evaluate, and possibly others are also meta-principles. Do they have analogous structure (universal property + mechanism children + outcome subsets)? Or is the structure specific to compression? Worth examination as those meta-principles get pulled forward.

5. **Discipline implication's load-bearingness.** The restructured directionality-and-surfacing ("name the error term and probe which parties it might become material to, across the relevant horizon") is more actionable than the original principle-#1 articulation. Is it actionable enough to propagate into operating constraints, or does it need further operationalization? Worth testing on real compression decisions the framework has produced.

6. **Recursive operationalized-minimum/core-insight applicability.** This restructure is itself an instance of the gap pattern applied recursively (materiality-mismatch was the previously-named pattern; the universal property is the core insight under it). Is the recursion itself a discipline pattern worth codifying — "when a sub-pattern is named, ask whether it's core-insight or operationalized-minimum of something more general"? Worth examining alongside any future principle-revision work.
