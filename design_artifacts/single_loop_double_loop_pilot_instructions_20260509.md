## Scope (in)

The deferred M will codify the single-loop vs double-loop learning frame (per Argyris/Schön) as an explicit framework discipline. The frame is currently named in spine prose (Entries 17, 19, 24 mentions; meta-AAR Phases 1 and 4 explicit application) but is not codified at the principle, glossary, or core-principles level. Codifying it makes "is this a single-loop tightening or a double-loop layer-add?" an explicit composition-time question on every future codification.

In scope:

- Single-loop / double-loop distinction at the framework-rules level (rules-within-which-the-system-operates vs rules-defining-the-system).
- Recognition criteria: when a recurring failure pattern signals that single-loop tightening has reached its limit and a double-loop response is needed.
- Composition-time application: whether the question "is this single-loop or double-loop" should be added to constraint #9 (verify-then-claim) or to a new constraint, or live in glossary, or both.
- Cross-reference work named in the translation table (validate, refine, or reject each candidate connection; codify confirmed connections).
- Worked instances from canonical state used as reference cases (Entries 17–20 verification ladder; Entry 24 paired versioning).

## Provenance and pressure-test instances

Three explicit double-loop instances are attested in canonical records:

- **Verification ladder construction (Entries 17–20).** Meta-AAR Phase 1 names the Argyris/Schön double-loop frame as the right read on this phase. The recurring multi-file-outcome overclaim pattern (Entries 1, 5, 12, 14, 16) was resistant to single-loop tightening (verify-then-claim, claim-versus-record divergence detection); the double-loop response was adding new layers (post-commit verification, hash-manifest, modification-confirmation). Each layer is a rules-of-frame change, not within-rules error correction.
- **Tapeworm-recursion + paired-versioning response (Entry 24).** Meta-AAR Phase 4 names this as the second load-bearing double-loop instance. Three-instance recurrence of mass-upload caching mismatch surfaced a structural gap that was not addressable by tightening prior verification layers; the response was to change the rules of file naming (timestamped spine + mod-N lateral versioning), bypassing the cache mechanism rather than diagnosing it.
- **Stakeholder operational pragmatism as double-loop trigger (Phase 2 Lessons).** Phase 2's diagnostic loop (Entries 21–22) ran longer than productive; the framework could not self-correct out of "diagnose more" without stakeholder pragmatism breaking the loop. The lesson generalizes: review-from-outside is a verification layer that reflexive-application cannot substitute for. This positions stakeholder pragmatism as a double-loop trigger when single-loop diagnostic loops cannot self-correct.

Additional candidate instances (Claim-status; see translation table) are not pre-judged; the deferred M validates them at execution.

## Falsifier framework

The codification fits if:

- The single-loop/double-loop distinction can be recognized at composition time on candidate codification work (the "is this S a single-loop tightening or a double-loop layer-add" question is answerable for actual S work).
- The three explicit instances above are correctly classified by the codified discipline.
- The candidate connections in the translation table can be validated, refined, or rejected against the codified discipline.

The codification doesn't fit if:

- The instances above turn out to be better described by a different frame (e.g., the recurring response pattern across Entries 17–20 is more accurately characterized as "layer addition" in a way that doesn't depend on the single-loop/double-loop distinction).
- The composition-time question proves unanswerable in practice (codifications turn out to be mostly ambiguous between single-loop and double-loop).
- Translation-table validation reveals that most Claim-status connections are stretches, undermining the frame's claim to recursive applicability across scales.

If falsified, the alternative is: keep the single-loop/double-loop frame in spine prose as a working concept but don't promote it to codified discipline. Document the attempted codification as a falsified-pass record (potentially a new artifact category — "falsified-pass record" would itself become a candidate framework move).
