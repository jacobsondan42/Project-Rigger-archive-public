# Glossary — Project Rigger

**Status:** Working reference. Consolidates terminology from `core_principles_for_agent_behavior.md`, `san_bruno_seed_export.md`, the handoff document, the library design document, the role definitions for onboarding and mentorship, and the job typology taxonomy developed in subsequent working sessions. New entries should be added in the same form: short prose definition, cross-references where useful. The glossary is a learning aid and a lookup reference; it is not the authoritative source for any concept (the source documents are).

---

**ACOM analogy / forced distribution.** Shorthand for the management-layer guardrail in the library design. The Army's officer evaluation system requires a Senior Rater to keep more than half of subordinates below "Above Center of Mass" — the math forbids universal top ratings. Translated to library contributions: a director whose contributed portfolio contains zero failures is more likely to be in the dark or curating their record than perfect, and the layer above (Alex, for the seven directors) is the right place to surface the distribution rather than the per-exemplar evaluation level. See also: *Survivorship bias*, *Bidirectionality*.

**After-Action Review (AAR).** Structured post-event review developed in the U.S. Army. Built around four questions: what was supposed to happen, what actually happened, why the difference, what to sustain or change. Separates description from judgment; treats lessons as institutional rather than personal. In Project Rigger, the AAR substrate (intent, outcome, why, lessons, skills) underlies projects, exemplars, learning-portfolio entries, executive sponsorship cycles, and the coordinator's synthesis function. Resume-bullet form is the public surface; the substrate is the durable record.

**Agent-as-interpret-layer.** Provisional candidate. The framework's principles assume the existence of an interpret-layer in the operational loop — capable of holding provenance and source-bias and confidence simultaneously, capable of pattern-surfacing across captures at different scales, capable of applying the framework's lenses recursively. The agent (Claude or successor) performs this interpret function; capture and evaluation are committed-to-record functions, while interpretation remains live work between captured material and human evaluation. Surfaced during the 2026-05-08 architecture pass on fractal application; pending integration as either an expansion of framework #15 or as a new structural acknowledgment of the framework's operational architecture. See also: *Capture-not-evaluate*, *Bidirectionality*.

**Allocative skill cluster.** The competency set specific to Allocative-kingdom work: decision authority under ambiguity, delegation mechanics, span-of-control calibration, accountability separation from personal performance, and coalition-building across positions the role doesn't directly control. Teachable through structured curriculum and accumulated reps in the same way any technical specialty is — does not develop automatically through technical competence or tenure. The military's officer sourcing programs (OCS, ROTC) are the clearest institutional acknowledgment that this skill cluster requires deliberate development rather than being assumed to arrive with promotion. In Rigger, the Allocative skill cluster is a first-class development domain: the learning library should have an explicit Allocative concept cluster, the progression graph should require demonstrated Allocative reps as prerequisites for edges that increase Allocative demand, and the AllocativeSkillRecord entity tracks those reps separately from general learning portfolio entries. See also: *Kingdom*, *Hat-stacking*, *Progression graph*.

**Analog participation.** The off-platform version of the onboarding/mentorship process for new hires who opt out of the tool. Structurally equivalent — the role-definition discipline applies, the conversations happen — but without the persistent-record layer. Not a fallback or degraded experience.

**Asymmetry-explicit framing.** A compression strategy that names the loss function rather than the number. Example: "I'm rounding up where it matters because being 1% over is recoverable and being 1% under is a Council problem." Particularly useful with audiences whose loss function tilts differently from the speaker's. See also: *Compression strategy*, *Loss aversion*.

**Attestation status.** Field on artifacts (manager-walkthrough notes, AARs, mentor service records) distinguishing *signed* (collaboratively reviewed and confirmed by both parties), *self-reported* (created by one party without the other's confirmation), and *self-reported-confirmed* (created by one party, later confirmed by the other). Permits the framework to function before universal manager participation while preserving the visibility of the attestation difference.

**Bidirectionality.** The principle that development obligation flows in both directions, not just downward. A development system that captures only what staff did with growth opportunities produces evaluation pressure flowing one way; a system that captures both what staff did *and* what executives sponsored produces shared obligation. Operationalized in executive sponsorship cycles, manager-portfolio review at the City Manager level, and CM-callout-as-trust-relevant-event. See also: *Trust-relevant events*, *Survivorship bias*.

**Big Five / OCEAN.** A personality framework measuring five continuous trait dimensions: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism. The most empirically validated personality framework available, with decades of peer-reviewed replication. Conscientiousness is the strongest single predictor of job performance across contexts; Neuroticism (low emotional stability) predicts performance degradation under stress and elevated turnover risk; Openness predicts learning acquisition and performance in novel-problem roles; Extraversion predicts performance in leadership and high-social-contact roles; Agreeableness predicts performance in team-dependent and service contexts. In Rigger, Big Five data belongs exclusively in the private utility-function layer with the most restrictive consent defaults. It must not enter the role evaluation layer, must not be joinable with standing function performance records, and carries a schema-level `layer_assignment` field permanently set to `utility_function` to enforce this structurally rather than operationally. See also: *HEXACO*, *Utility function*, *Role versus utility function*, *IndividualCharacteristicRecord*.

**Buddy.** Peer-level onboarding role; same or adjacent department; daily check-in for week 1, tapering to as-needed. Day-to-day navigation function — printer location, PO process, who handles what. Distinct from the mentor (cross-departmental, portfolio demonstrator) and the coordinator (cross-cohort, synthesis function). No portfolio commitment, no AAR-eligibility by default; the value depends on the casual register that formal recordkeeping would erode.

**Byte-clean base.** Small framework composition-time discipline: the stakeholder uploads a known-clean canonical-state copy of any fragile reference file to `/mnt/user-data/uploads/` at session start (or before composition of an entry that will modify that file), so Claude composes against verified bytes rather than reconstructing the file from project_knowledge_search chunks or conversation history. Avoids the failure class of reconstruction noise — small drift in structure, formatting, or phrasing that would otherwise compound across entries as Claude rebuilds from approximate sources. Three-instance pressure-tested at session boundaries before codification: Entry 21 first instance applying the discipline to `cohort_temporal_record.md` (per Entry 21 lesson #6); Entry 22 second instance same file (Entry 22 lesson #5); Entry 23 third instance same file (Entry 23 lesson #5). Mechanism-agnostic by design: the discipline doesn't claim what specific failure mode reconstruction noise produces (small wording drift, structural drift, missed sections, etc.); it just names the prep discipline that structurally avoids the class regardless. Distinct from Entry 24's timestamped-spine + mod-N-lateral versioning paired discipline (which addresses transmission-layer caching at upload-batch granularity) by addressing a different layer (composition-time reconstruction). The two disciplines are complementary: byte-clean base ensures Claude is composing from the right bytes; paired versioning ensures the produced bytes land canonically. Verify-then-redrop on the static-named README is the related composition-time + transmission-time discipline pair for the entry-point file specifically. Codified as Entry 25 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Tapeworm recursion*, *Mod-N versioning*, *Timestamped spine*, *Rename-as-navigation*.

**Case study.** Narrative writeup of how a situation was approached, shaped for a reader who wasn't there. Less raw than an exemplar — enough framing to make decisions legible. The natural form for City Manager all-hands callouts and Council recognition because they tell a story rather than dropping a thirty-page report on the audience. See also: *Exemplar*, *CM-callout surface*.

**Character sheet.** First of the three structured onboarding artifacts; produced through the day-one self-exercise. Covers self-reported context, preferences, constraints (with private-basis-public-output handling), and the new hire's own framing of the role they are stepping into. See also: *Position portfolio*, *Manager-walkthrough notes*.

**CM-callout surface.** The City Manager's recognition function within the library — the channel by which Alex pulls case studies for all-hands or Council recognition. Designed bidirectionally: the act of being called out flows back to the contributor's portfolio as a trust-relevant event. Not a surveillance surface; the dashboard organizes around case studies authored for visibility, not raw artifacts.

**Co-equal session limiters.** Small framework cadence discipline: each conversation thread (session) is governed by two co-equal limiters that fire whichever-comes-first within the thread, with both resetting at every new thread since the remedy in either case is a new thread. The two limiters: (a) the *effort accumulator*, capped at 15 units per thread under the existing 1/3/5 (S/M/L) effort-tagging discipline (per the `Cadence patterns at multiple scales` subsection of `framework_decisions_record.md` codified in Entry 15); (b) the *handoff-instance counter*, capped at N=1 — at most one chain-entry handoff per thread, since each chain entry produces one handoff and producing more than one within the same thread accumulates intra-thread drift in working context that the handoff protocol cannot cleanly transmit to a fresh session's working memory. The cross-session handshake protocol carries forward the in-progress work via the recommended-opener mechanism (component #4) — when a limiter fires, the new thread starts on a clean handoff and the work continues there. Out-of-chain session-closes (Entry 28's codified shape) do not advance the handoff-instance counter beyond zero, since they don't produce chain entries — out-of-chain sessions can be arbitrarily long without firing the handoff-instance limiter (the effort accumulator still applies if the session does enough non-chain work). The handoff-instance counter is conceptually distinct from the cumulative pressure-test count used for codification candidacy: cumulative pressure-test asks "how many times has this discipline been observed across all threads ever?" (informs codification at the ~3-instance recurrence threshold) and does not reset; handoff-instance asks "how many chain-entry handoffs has this thread produced?" (informs session closure at N=1) and resets per thread. Conflating the two produced count drift in Entries 27 and 28's claimed "fifteenth instance" / "sixteenth instance" of the filename convention (off-by-1 high in each case; recorded as chain audit dimension move's second canonical instance in Entry 29's outcome and skills fields). Under this discipline, the framework's "active codification arc" framing — multi-thread accumulator running cumulatively to 15/15 across multiple threads — is superseded; arcs are per-thread, and inter-thread codification work is opportunistic rather than arc-committed. Codification operationalizes the *per-entry-produced cap as co-equal cadence trigger* candidate carried since Entry 18's close handoff (`handoff_20260509T212404Z.md`) for ten subsequent entries (Entries 19–28), finally resolved in Entry 29 with the stakeholder's confirming direction. Codified as Entry 29 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Out-of-chain session-close*, *Handoff hash manifest*, *Timestamped filename convention*, *Cross-session handshake protocol*.

**Cohort.** Group of new hires sharing a 90-day onboarding window, organized by hire quarter. Provides cross-departmental social tissue, trust-relevant events at scale, and a distributed-accountability frame for executive sponsorship. The cohort is a logistical envelope around the 1:1 structural work, not a replacement for it.

**Compounding.** Percentage changes applied serially produce results different from the sum of those percentages. In multi-period contract analysis, the difference between simple and compounded figures can shift the political reading of a deal substantially. A three-year contract with 6% annual wage increases is 18% over the term simple but 19.1% compounded. Choosing which to present is a value judgment about which audience and failure mode is being prioritized. See also: *Compression strategy*.

**Compression strategy.** The choice of how to present complex information given audience, purpose, and failure mode. Several patterns are available — *layered presentation*, *default-and-flag*, *asymmetry-explicit framing*, *range with a decision trigger* — and the right one depends on whose utility function is being served. The principle: surface the lens before the number. Choosing without saying which is being chosen is the failure mode.

**Concept (library).** Underlying idea inside one or more syllabus modules — "fully burdened cost," "fit drift," "transaction cost of outsourcing." Concepts sit beneath modules but cross module boundaries and are the retrieval primitive for concept-lookup queries in the library. Each concept carries a `primary_kingdom` tag and a `substrate` tag, enabling concept-lookup queries to be filtered by kingdom and enabling gap analysis — concepts that are present in the syllabi but have no exemplars yet, organized by kingdom rather than just by module. See also: *Module*, *Exemplar*, *Kingdom*.

**Constraints-masking.** Architecture for handling protected information (medical, legal, family-care, etc.) where the basis is private but operational implications need to be surfaceable. Private-basis / public-output: an operator can communicate "I'm constrained on Tuesdays" without surfacing why. The constraints-masking section of the day-one self-exercise must be skippable at any granularity, with conservative privacy defaults and visible privacy mechanisms.

**Coordinator / synthesizer.** Cross-cohort onboarding role producing synthesis upward. Holds five-minute weekly check-ins with active new hires, captures observations, synthesizes findings into individual / manager / cohort-pattern outputs. Filled by Dan in v1 with the explicit intent that the role transitions to agent-augmented in v2 — a tool-plus-process rather than a personal availability. See also: *Buddy*, *Mentor*.

**Cross-organizational mentor.** Mentorship relationship outside the formal reporting structure and outside the onboarding window. Different trigger and cadence from the onboarding mentor. Recorded on both sides (mentee and mentor portfolios) with appropriate privacy controls. Mentor service is AAR-eligible.

**Default-and-flag.** A compression strategy: "I'm presenting compounded — say so if you'd rather see straight-line." Lighter than layering; makes the assumption explicit enough to be challenged without slowing the conversation.

**Diagnose-before-codify.** Small framework principle: when discipline architecture detects a recurring failure but the underlying cause is not yet known, defer codification of any response that *claims a specific mechanism* until the cause is identified — premature mechanism-claiming codification can be wrong-shaped and lock the framework into an incorrect response. The principle protects against confidently codifying "X is the fragility response" before X is confirmed as the mechanism. Importantly, the principle does NOT protect against *mechanism-agnostic* navigation moves: renaming a file, retrying with backoff, adding redundancy — these don't claim what mechanism is responsible and remain operationally available regardless of diagnostic state. The scope-clarification matters because without it the principle could be misapplied to indefinitely defer all codification when persistent failure has unclear cause, substituting analytical completeness for operational responsiveness. Codified as Entry 23 of `framework_decisions_record.md` and as principle #22 in `framework_principles.md`; two-instance pressure-tested across Entries 21–22 with Entry 23's rename-pivot as the mechanism-agnostic exit. Applies symmetrically to author-side and stakeholder-side proposed responses (a stakeholder's good-faith mechanism-claiming workaround is itself subject to the principle; a stakeholder's mechanism-agnostic navigation proposal is not). See also: *Rename-as-navigation*, *Mechanical-verification-of-claims-catches-precision-overclaims*.

**Discipline architecture validation.** Small framework property naming what's been observed about the framework's discipline architecture (per Entry 24's paired-versioning + lazy-instantiation discipline) holding operationally at scale. Six confirming instances at session openers (`verify_entry_N.py` reporting clean PASS without single-file re-drop on the static-named README), including across three consecutive out-of-chain session boundaries: Entry 24→25 opener (1st); Entry 25→26 opener after stakeholder Path A resolution to claim-versus-record divergence detected at Stage 2 opener (2nd); Entry 26→Eric-onboarding-session opener per `handoff_20260510T043000Z.md` (3rd); Eric-onboarding→recommendation-only opener per `handoff_20260510T060000Z.md` (4th); recommendation-only→fresh-playground opener per `handoff_20260510T080000Z.md` (5th); fresh-playground→Entry 27 opener with both out-of-chain artifacts (`single_loop_double_loop_pilot_instructions_20260509.md` + `single_loop_double_loop_translation_table_20260510T080000Z.md`) present in canonical state without disturbing in-chain validation (6th, the first instance under the new condition that out-of-chain artifacts sit alongside the chain). The architecture predicts (a) timestamped-spine + mod-N-lateral versioning structurally bypass tapeworm-recursion exposure for the spine and lateral reference files (each modification produces a fresh filename, so whatever the cache is keyed on, the new version is not at the keyed path); (b) the verify-then-redrop tax on the static-named README is the necessary cost of keeping the entry-point legible while the rest of the architecture structurally bypasses tapeworm exposure (README must remain at a stable path to be findable as the entry point; the verify routine catches any failure-to-land at session-opener time and single-file re-drop bypasses the batch-level cache per the tapeworm-recursion finding's mechanism-agnostic response shape from Entry 24); (c) the architecture is robust to session-shape variation (the six instances span in-chain transitions and three consecutive out-of-chain session boundaries, holding stable across both). Distinguishes the structural status of currently-static-named files: README is *deliberately* static-named for entry-point legibility and incurs the verify-then-redrop tax permanently under the architecture; `framework_principles.md` and `core_principles_for_agent_behavior.md` are *incidentally* static-named under Entry 24's lazy-instantiation discipline (not yet modified post-discipline-adoption) and currently incur no tapeworm exposure because no re-upload occurs across entries — they will mod-N on first modification, joining the lateral category and gaining structural tapeworm bypass at that point. The codification is mechanism-agnostic per the diagnose-before-codify scope-clarification: it names what's been observed and what the architecture predicts, without claiming the underlying tapeworm-recursion mechanism (which remains undiagnosed). Codified as Entry 27 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. The codification entry's own clean drop at next session's opener will be the seventh confirming instance, materializing the reflexive-validation-by-application property of the codification's pass-shape (small standalone codification + reflexive validation by application — seventh distinct pass-shape category in the framework's pass-shape lexicon under the Entry 25 codified counting convention). See also: *Tapeworm recursion*, *Mod-N versioning*, *Timestamped spine*, *Rename-as-navigation*, *Diagnose-before-codify*.

**Distributional effects.** The pattern of how gains and losses fall across stakeholder groups, separately from aggregate net effect. Standard cost-benefit analysis can show positive net utility while concentrating losses on a small group. Surfacing distributional effects ahead of decisions is the difference between informed political choice and accidental redistribution.

**Emergent-fractal.** Provisional candidate. Scale-behavior shape: principle is fractal in operation but anti-linear in claim — the principle's content is precisely that scales DON'T reduce to lower-level components; higher-scale dynamics are non-linear in the components. Cohort-level dynamics (#17) is the canonical instance. Structural inverse of hierarchical-fractal (emergent rather than composed). Provisional pending integration. See also: *Flat-fractal*, *Hierarchical-fractal*, *Proportional-fractal*.

**Exemplar.** Real artifact produced in the course of city work — a staff report, a deep dive, a fully-burdened-cost analysis, a negotiation framing memo. Tagged with the concepts it instantiates, attributed to the people who produced it, and carrying the AAR substrate from the original work. Distinct from a case study (which is shaped narrative) and from an external reference (which is from outside the city). See also: *Case study*, *Concept*, *External reference*.

**Executive sponsor.** Rotating role across the seven directors (excluding the City Manager), two months per year per executive. Hosts cohort welcome event, participates in at least one cohort touch point during window, AAR-eligible at cycle end per the bidirectionality principle. The sponsorship cycle is itself an Allocative-kingdom standing function — it requires resource direction, decision-making under ambiguity about which cohort moments deserve attention, and accountability for outcomes the sponsor doesn't personally control. The ACOM-style guardrail applies at the portfolio level: an executive whose every cycle reads as surface-level engagement produces a portfolio shape the reviewing layer should notice. See also: *Bidirectionality*, *Trust-relevant events*, *Allocative skill cluster*.

**External reference.** Material from outside the city referenced in the library — GFOA guides, CSMFO templates, ICMA materials, books, articles, the syllabi the program is built around. Lower priority than inside-produced material because the library's purpose is elevating what the city itself does, but useful as supporting context.

**Fit drift.** The gradual divergence between what a role requires and what the person occupying it brings, as either the role or the person evolves. Most executive performance issues are fit drift surfaced too late rather than capability failures. Fit drift has at least two distinct forms. *Performance-against-role fit drift* is the gap between what the role requires and what the person currently brings — a person-performance question. *Kingdom-composition fit drift* is the gap between what the role's formal accountability describes and what its actual standing functions demand — a role-design question. The two can occur simultaneously but lead to different conversations and interventions. A system that cannot distinguish them will systematically misattribute organizational problems to individual inadequacy. The framework's value is partly in catching both forms early enough that the conversation can happen before either becomes a crisis. See also: *Kingdom-composition drift*, *Kingdom composition*.

**Flat-fractal.** Provisional candidate. Scale-behavior shape: same operation recurs at each scale; scales are independent. Compression at sentence/paragraph/document/conversation/channel scales is the canonical instance. Most framework principles fall under this shape per the 2026-05-08 architecture pass. Distinguished from hierarchical-fractal (where scales depend on each other) and proportional-fractal (where the asymmetry between scales is the principle's content). Provisional pending integration. See also: *Hierarchical-fractal*, *Proportional-fractal*, *Emergent-fractal*.

**Goodhart's Law.** Generalized from the economist Charles Goodhart: when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Once people understand they are being evaluated against a proxy, they optimize for the proxy rather than the underlying thing. Standard remedies aim at preventing the optimization rather than reversing it after the fact: auditing, varying the measures, keeping outputs provisional, periodic review.

**Handoff hash manifest.** Small framework composition discipline: each session-close handoff that pairs with canonical-state file production embeds the SHA-256 hashes of those files in human-readable form (filename → hash), mirroring the `EXPECTED_HASHES` dict at the top of the corresponding `verify_entry_N.py` script. The two artifacts derive from the same composition-time fingerprint capture; any divergence between handoff manifest and verify-script manifest is mechanical rather than interpretive, and either surface can serve as the canonical fingerprint for sanity-checking the other. Closes a single-surface-drift mode: when only the verify script carries the manifest, the human-readable handoff cannot be sanity-checked against canonical state without running the script; when only the handoff carries the manifest, the verify script cannot mechanically detect transmission drift. The two surfaces together let the next session sanity-check claimed canonical state by reading the handoff or running the script — divergence between the surfaces is itself diagnostic information. Pattern instantiated alongside Entry 19's hash-manifest discipline (post-commit verification's Layer 1) and present in every chain-entry session-close handoff since `handoff_20260509T215438Z.md`. Eleven-instance cumulative pressure-test through `handoff_20260510T120000Z.md` (Entry 28's close) at the time of Entry 29 codification — counting chain-entry session-close handoffs from Entry 19's close through Entry 28's close, since out-of-chain session-close handoffs do not produce fresh canonical state and carry the prior chain-entry session's manifest forward by reference rather than fresh embedding. The discipline is the third canonical instance of hash-mechanical linkage as a recursively-applicable framework-level pattern per Entry 20's lessons (chain-hash integrity since genesis; verify-script `EXPECTED_HASHES` from Entry 19; handoff hash manifest as the human-readable surface of the same fingerprint), supporting the recursive-applicability reading without addressing the recursive-applicability claim itself (which remains a separately-tracked codification candidate on the deferred-M translation table row 5). Codified as Entry 29 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Hash-manifest discipline* (Entry 19 baseline within the post-commit verification routine subsection of `framework_decisions_record.md`), *Modification-confirmation discipline* (Entry 20 baseline, complementary `PRIOR_HASHES`), *Timestamped filename convention*, *Cross-session handshake protocol*.

**Hat-stacking.** The condition in which a single role simultaneously carries standing functions from multiple kingdoms, which is the normal condition for most senior individual contributor and managerial roles rather than an exception. The IC-to-manager transition is better described as a shift in the proportion of Allocative demand within a portfolio of hat-stacked functions than as a binary kingdom crossing. This matters for how fit drift is diagnosed and how progression graph edges are designed: moving from IC to manager is not a crossing from zero Allocative work to full Allocative accountability, but a shift in proportion — more manageable if the person has been accumulating Allocative reps in the predecessor role, more abrupt if those reps were systematically withheld or absent. Senior IC roles should explicitly include some Allocative standing functions as a matter of classification design, not as an exception, so that the progression graph edge to management reflects a proportional increase rather than a discontinuous leap. See also: *Allocative skill cluster*, *Kingdom composition*, *Progression graph*.

**Help-wanted surface.** Library/dashboard channel for niche, novel, or lingering problems where someone wants input. The flag shares the project-as-AAR-level-object — original goal, what's changed, current ask — so anyone responding has the context of the work. Cross-organizational by design; one of the mechanisms by which mentorship and cross-functional knowledge-sharing actually happen.

**HEXACO.** A six-factor extension of the Big Five personality framework adding Honesty-Humility to the standard five dimensions. The Honesty-Humility factor predicts counterproductive work behaviors, integrity violations, and trust-relevant outcomes better than any Big Five factor alone, making it particularly relevant in a public-accountability context where the gap between stated commitments and observed behavior is organizationally and politically significant. For Rigger, carries the same consent architecture and layer restrictions as Big Five: private utility-function layer, most restrictive default, not joinable with role evaluation records. See also: *Big Five / OCEAN*, *IndividualCharacteristicRecord*.

**Hierarchical-fractal.** Provisional candidate. Scale-behavior shape: same operation recurs at each scale, but higher-scale instances depend on lower-scale instances having accumulated. Trust as rate-limiter is the canonical instance — cohort trust depends on individual trust having accumulated, organizational trust depends on cohort trust, etc. Sponsorship-staged (#6) shares this shape. Distinguished from flat-fractal (independent scales) and emergent-fractal (non-linear scale composition). Provisional pending integration. See also: *Flat-fractal*, *Proportional-fractal*, *Emergent-fractal*.

**Holland RIASEC.** A psychometrically validated framework organizing occupational environments and individual orientations into six types: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional. Person-environment congruence on Holland codes predicts tenure, satisfaction, and job performance, and the framework has one of the longest empirical records of any occupational fit instrument. Adjacent to the job typology taxonomy's Kingdom/Phylum structure — both are organizing work by what kind of engagement it requires — but designed for broad occupational fit across the labor market rather than position-level specificity within a single organization. See also: *Job typology taxonomy*, *Kingdom*.

**Informed consent.** First-class entity in the data model. Recorded at day-one self-exercise (and continuously thereafter), scoped per category (visibility to manager, to cohort, to City Manager, to library, etc.), revocable, timestamped. No layer of the platform activates without consent for that layer specifically. Default is most-restrictive; expansion of visibility is by explicit per-category consent rather than blanket opt-in.

**Job typology taxonomy.** Diagnostic framework organizing work by relationship to value, structured analogously to biological taxonomy. Domain (Material/Symbolic — the substrate of work), Kingdom (Generative, Mediative, Interpretive, Protective, Allocative — how the work relates to value), Phylum (substrate the work acts on: physical goods, capital, information, people, systems), Class (primary instrument: measurement, narrative, force/authority, relationship, fabrication, procedure), then Order, Family, Genus, and Species down to job title. Functions as a meta-structure over Rigger's operational classification system: Rigger's function families describe what domain the work is in; the taxonomy describes what the work *does* to value and what it acts on. Most useful upstream of data capture — for interpreting fit drift and kingdom-composition drift when the operational layer surfaces them, for calibrating what development support is appropriate for which progression edges, and for understanding why cross-director friction often reflects kingdom-level differences in how people read shared priorities rather than personality conflict. See also: *Kingdom*, *Kingdom composition*, *Fit drift*, *Holland RIASEC*, *Requisite Organization*.

**Kingdom.** The primary level of the job typology taxonomy, defining how a role or standing function relates to value. Five kingdoms: *Generative* (creates new value where none existed — builders, engineers, planners, artists), *Mediative* (facilitates value exchange between parties — traders, coordinators, logistics, matchmakers), *Interpretive* (represents, translates, or renders value legible — analysts, accountants, educators, journalists), *Protective* (preserves value against degradation, loss, or threat — physicians, lawyers, inspectors, archivists), *Allocative* (directs value toward ends, determining who or what gets what — managers, executives, planners, commanders). Kingdom assignment belongs at the standing function level, not the position level, because most senior positions carry standing functions from multiple kingdoms simultaneously. See also: *Kingdom composition*, *Hat-stacking*, *Job typology taxonomy*.

**Kingdom composition.** The distribution of a position's standing functions across the five kingdoms, expressed as proportions. The position-fit cross-tab should surface kingdom-composition mismatches between actual portfolio (derived from standing function `primary_kingdom` and `kingdom_composition` tags) and nominal classification profile (the `ClassificationKingdomProfile` the classification was designed around). A position with substantially more Allocative demand than its classification profiles describes may indicate an under-classified role performing director-equivalent work; substantially less may indicate role erosion. Both are organizationally significant and neither is a simple performance question. See also: *Kingdom-composition drift*, *Position-fit cross-tab*, *Hat-stacking*.

**Kingdom-composition drift.** The gradual divergence between the kingdom composition the role's formal accountability describes and the kingdom composition of its actual standing functions. Distinct from performance-against-role fit drift, which is a person-performance question. Kingdom-composition drift is a role-design question — the position has evolved away from what its classification describes — and leads to different interventions. A director whose Allocative standing functions have steadily been absorbed by a deputy while they accumulate Interpretive and Generative work may be personally thriving while creating structural organizational risk: formal accountability hasn't moved with the actual work, and that gap surfaces badly when the deputy leaves or when Council asks a question the director no longer owns the answer to. The `PositionKingdomProfile` entity and the cross-tab extension track this over time. See also: *Fit drift*, *Kingdom composition*, *PositionKingdomProfile*.

**Layered presentation.** A compression strategy: headline number first, then alternative views. Lets the audience anchor on a synthesis without losing the choice of lens. Most useful when the audience has a clear preference and the alternatives are confirming context.

**Learning portfolio.** Position-attached layer parallel to standing functions and projects. Module engagement, completion, and planned engagement carry the same AAR substrate as projects. Skills graph populates from all three sources (functions, projects, learning) with provenance tagged. The Leadership Academy, Analyst Boot Camp, Core Supervision, and Make vs Buy syllabi are parallel development infrastructure that integrates at the architecture level — not content libraries the framework references, but parallel structures.

**Loss aversion.** The empirical finding from Kahneman and Tversky that losses register about twice as heavily as equivalent gains in human decision-making. **Requisite Organization / Stratified Systems Theory.** Elliott Jaques' framework organizing organizational roles by the time horizon of accountable decision-making rather than by headcount or salary grade. **Tapeworm recursion.** The mass-upload static-name caching mechanism observed empirically in the project knowledge upload pipeline: when a batch of files is uploaded together and some files share names with prior uploads, some [0, n] of the static-named files retain their cached prior version rather than the freshly-dropped version. Single-file re-drops of affected files land canonically, suggesting the mechanism is keyed on batch-level deduplication or staleness behavior with respect to already-existing filenames rather than on file content or specific-filename fragility. The mechanism's underlying cause remains undiagnosed — the name captures the observed pattern (segmented uploads where some segments are stale prior versions, evocative of how tapeworm proglottids replicate with some segments being copies of an older one) without claiming what the cache is keyed on. Per the diagnose-before-codify principle (#22), mechanism-agnostic responses remain available regardless of diagnostic state: the framework's response is mod-N versioning of major lateral reference files plus timestamped-spine versioning of the chain entry file (see those entries). Detected via `verify_entry_N.py` hash mismatches at session pickup; remediable via single-file re-drop. Search-index-vs-filesystem divergence is a related observation surface — the search index appears to process new uploads independently of whatever filesystem-snapshot caching is happening, so the two views drift on exactly the files where the cache served stale content. Three-instance recurrence across Entries 22, 23, 24 openers (Entry 22 opener cohort_temporal_record.md; Entry 23 opener cohort_temporal_record.md again; Entry 24 opener project_rigger_glossary.md — the third instance on a previously-unfragile file isolated the mechanism via single-file re-drop landing clean, falsifying the file-specific filename-keyed hypothesis and unifying with the search-index-vs-filesystem divergence pattern as same mechanism viewed from two surfaces). Codified as Entry 24 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Mod-N versioning*, *Timestamped spine*, *Diagnose-before-codify*, *Rename-as-navigation*.

**Timestamped filename convention.** Small framework composition discipline: file types whose canonical durability is keyed on temporal ordering use a UTC ISO 8601 compact filename suffix `_YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.md`. Original baseline: handoff documents per Entry 18 codification (`handoff_YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.md`, established 2026-05-09 in `handoff_20260509T204202Z.md`). The convention's load-bearing properties: (a) lexicographic sort on filename equals chronological sort, since UTC ISO 8601 compact format is sort-stable; (b) every new instantiation produces a fresh filename, providing automatic tapeworm-recursion bypass per Entry 24 (no batch-upload cache key collision possible across timestamps); (c) the `Z` suffix encodes the timezone explicitly so PT/UTC drift across the framework's working sessions doesn't introduce ambiguity. The convention is what lets future file types added to the chain framework (e.g. retrospective documents, audit notes, alongside-chain artifacts that warrant timestamp-keyed durability such as `single_loop_double_loop_translation_table_20260510T080000Z.md`) adopt the same property without re-codification — name the convention once at glossary level, and any subsequent file type can apply it by following the suffix pattern. Codified as Entry 29 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Timestamped spine* (Entry 24 application to the chain entry file), *Recursive (handoff_T, spine_T) pairing* (Entry 24 baseline, depends on the convention being applied identically to both files), *Tapeworm recursion* (mechanism the fresh filenames bypass), *Co-equal session limiters* (cumulative-vs-per-thread distinction), *Cross-session handshake protocol* (component #2 in `README.md` documents the convention's handoff application).

**Timestamped spine.** Small framework discipline: the chain entry file (historically `framework_decisions_record.md`) is versioned along its temporal axis as `framework_decisions_record_<TIMESTAMP>.md`, paired with the existing `handoff_<TIMESTAMP>.md` naming convention (UTC ISO 8601 compact format). The recursive pairing extends the chain-hash integrity property (each entry references predecessor by hash) across the directional axis as well as the state axis. Tapeworm resistance is automatic since every timestamp produces a fresh filename. Distinct from mod-N versioning (which versions lateral reference files along an integer axis); timestamped spine is for the chain entry file specifically because its temporal ordering is intrinsic and its pairing with handoffs is structural. The currently-static `framework_decisions_record.md` becomes orphaned at its current state at the discipline's first instantiation (Entry 24) and remains as historical fossil per the chain's append-only discipline. Recovery at any timestamp T from the (handoff_T, spine_T) pair is fully deterministic for chain state; full state recovery is strictly deterministic when the lateral-file mod manifest is included in handoff_T (per the handoff-as-state-pointer extension in Entry 24's discipline). Codified as Entry 24 of `framework_decisions_record.md` and as Cross-session handshake protocol component #8 in `README.md`; first canonical instance is `framework_decisions_record_20260510T010156Z.md`. **Whose utility function.** The framing question that runs beneath any compression decision. **Working Genius / WIDGET.** Lencioni's model identifying six types of work required across a project lifecycle: Wonder (sensing gaps, asking generative questions), Invention (creating novel solutions), Discernment (evaluating with judgment), Galvanizing (rallying people toward action), Enablement (supporting and filling gaps), Tenacity (pushing through to completion). Each person has two Working Geniuses (energizing and natural), two Working Competencies (adequate), two Working Frustrations (draining). Maps roughly onto kingdoms: Wonder and Invention are early-phase Generative; Discernment maps to Interpretive; Galvanizing maps to Allocative; Enablement maps to Mediative and Protective; Tenacity is late-phase across kingdoms. In Rigger, useful as conversation scaffold for the utility function layer rather than as a classification instrument — the validation evidence does not support load-bearing use. Best accumulated from AAR substrate reflection prompts over time (which phases were most energizing, which required the most compensatory effort) rather than from a single self-report assessment. Complements Big Five: the Big Five describes stable behavioral tendencies that don't change much across contexts; Working Genius describes phase-level energization within a project. Together they give a more complete picture of utility function than either provides alone. See also: *Utility function*, *AAR substrate*, *Big Five / OCEAN*, *Kingdom*.
