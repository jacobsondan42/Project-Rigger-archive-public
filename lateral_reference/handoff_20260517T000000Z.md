**Substantive findings from the framework read of Dan Z's drafts** (preserved here for fold-in to any future chain entry that codifies the §L work):

- **Three-axis narrowing of revised PSPM.** The revised PSPM narrows on three independent axes relative to the original: specialty-aggregation (sheds quant methods / fund accounting depth / labor relations specifics → generic public-admin equivalents), temporal-horizon ("current and near-term needs" + "long-term goals" → "long-term strategic objectives" only), and role-type oscillation ("either a manager or practitioner" → manager/advisor only). All three axes move in the same direction. The original PSPM was the maximum-envelope-width single-class spec in the corpus to date; the revised version narrows substantially.

- **Practitioner-mode migration to SSPM.** The original PSPM's most distinctive role-design feature (oscillation between manager and practitioner depending on assignment) has not been preserved in the revised PSPM; it has migrated downward to SSPM. The SSPM Definition keeps the "identifies needs, develops alternatives and implementation plans, and executes selected solutions" + "supplementary capacity" language verbatim from the original PSPM. Real-world implication: the current PSPM incumbent's marquee work (Tyler Tech ERP implementation per the Council packet) is an executing-the-selected-path activity, which under the new classification family fits SSPM cleaner than the revised PSPM.

- **Altitude promotion without breadth expansion in revised PSPM.** Original spec: "same level of independence and judgement as department heads." Revised spec: "comparable to department heads" + new clause "expected to influence organizational priorities and outcomes." Reporting line rises (under ASD/ACM/designee → under CM/designee). Narrower in breadth, higher in altitude — aligned with Grade 7 placement in the Council packet (department-head peer).

- **Differentiated-body vs shared-body design philosophy.** SB SPM is differentiated-body (each level has its own Essential Duties); Hayward MA I/II/Senior is shared-body (identical Essential Duties, level differentiation via autonomy/supervision framing). Contrast pair for the rubric — same nominal pattern (three-tier intra-agency series) at different design philosophies.

- **Drafting residuals in Dan Z's drafts.** SPM Ability-To section is verbatim PSPM Ability-To (unedited copy); SPM Supervision-Exercised is wider than SSPM's (non-monotonic across the series); SPM has copy-stage typos ("technicaland", "organiztional", "Establishs"); revised PSPM Working Knowledge has broken bullet formatting.

### §J (rubric-spec downstream work) — seven sub-item advancements from the SB SPM pressure-test pass

**J.1 — Multi-axis envelope-width attribute.** Replace scalar `envelope_width_estimate: narrow | medium | wide` with `envelope_axes: {specialty_aggregation, temporal_horizon, role_type_oscillation, altitude}` (axis list non-exhaustive; further axes admissible as evidence accumulates). **J.2 — Intra-document consistency check as explicit rubric procedural step.** *Stakeholder-flagged priority.* Add a step between segmentation and function-extraction in the rubric procedure that compares the Definition / Distinguishing Characteristics / Essential Duties / KSA-Working-Knowledge / Ability-To sections for tier-consistency. If they do not agree on the level of work, surface a structured inconsistency note rather than averaging or arbitrarily privileging one section. Evidence: SB SPM draft demonstrates the failure mode at scale — SPM's Ability-To section was verbatim PSPM-tier ("Advise executive leadership," "Lead high-impact, cross-departmental initiatives," "individual contributor to strategic lead") while the rest of the SPM was junior-tier ("assists in identifying needs," "work is typically performed on assigned projects with defined parameters," "coordinates and supports cross-departmental projects"). A rubric without intra-document consistency check would either average across the inconsistency (producing a mid-tier composite that matches no section) or arbitrarily privilege one section (producing a directional answer dependent on which section is privileged). The underlying pattern (drafting residuals leaving sections at inconsistent tiers) is plausibly common in draft-state specs and adjacent-revision specs, so re-pressure-tests likely to come quickly. **Pressure-test depth:** one-instance this thread; expected to accumulate fast. **Codification priority:** highest among J advancements per stakeholder direction.

**J.3 — Series-design-philosophy attribute.** Add `series_design_philosophy: shared_body | differentiated_body | hybrid` at the series-rollup level (above individual JDDecompositionRecord). Evidence: Hayward = shared_body (Entry 37 worked example); SB SPM = differentiated_body. The two cases require different decomposition treatments and currently the rubric has no place to record the distinction. **Pressure-test depth:** two-instance (Hayward + SB SPM).

**J.4 — Spec-authoring mode attribute.** Add `spec_authoring_mode: incumbent_fit | structural_slot | unknown` at the JDDecompositionRecord level. Evidence: SB PSPM original = incumbent_fit (KSAs include specific items like "neural networks and machine learning," fund-accounting period-close depth, labor-relations proposal-costing — read as a specific person's profile, not generic public-admin requirements). SB PSPM revised + SSPM + SPM = structural_slot (KSAs scoped to role design). The original-to-revised diff in this thread is itself empirical evidence of the distinction. **J.5 — Role-mode attribute.** Add `role_mode: practitioner | manager | oscillating | unknown` at the JDDecompositionRecord level.
