# Glossary — Project Rigger

**Status:** Working reference. Consolidates terminology from `core_principles_for_agent_behavior.md`, `san_bruno_seed_export.md`, the handoff document, the library design document, the role definitions for onboarding and mentorship, and the job typology taxonomy developed in subsequent working sessions. New entries should be added in the same form: short prose definition, cross-references where useful. The glossary is a learning aid and a lookup reference; it is not the authoritative source for any concept (the source documents are).

**ACOM analogy / forced distribution.** Shorthand for the management-layer guardrail in the library design. The Army's officer evaluation system requires a Senior Rater to keep more than half of subordinates below "Above Center of Mass" — the math forbids universal top ratings. Translated to library contributions: a director whose contributed portfolio contains zero failures is more likely to be in the dark or curating their record than perfect, and the layer above (Alex, for the seven directors) is the right place to surface the distribution rather than the per-exemplar evaluation level. See also: *Survivorship bias*, *Bidirectionality*.

**After-Action Review (AAR).** Structured post-event review developed in the U.S. Army. Built around four questions: what was supposed to happen, what actually happened, why the difference, what to sustain or change. Separates description from judgment; treats lessons as institutional rather than personal. In Project Rigger, the AAR substrate (intent, outcome, why, lessons, skills) underlies projects, exemplars, learning-portfolio entries, executive sponsorship cycles, and the coordinator's synthesis function. Resume-bullet form is the public surface; the substrate is the durable record.

**Agent-as-interpret-layer.** Provisional candidate. The framework's principles assume the existence of an interpret-layer in the operational loop — capable of holding provenance and source-bias and confidence simultaneously, capable of pattern-surfacing across captures at different scales, capable of applying the framework's lenses recursively. The agent (Claude or successor) performs this interpret function; capture and evaluation are committed-to-record functions, while interpretation remains live work between captured material and human evaluation. Surfaced during the 2026-05-08 architecture pass on fractal application; pending integration as either an expansion of framework #15 or as a new structural acknowledgment of the framework's operational architecture. See also: *Capture-not-evaluate*, *Bidirectionality*.

**Aggregation-horizon classification.** Small framework records-keeping discipline (one of five operational components of *Observational discipline*): the productive-vs-failure classification of any compression cannot be made at the instance horizon; it can only be made at the aggregation horizon, because the failure mode that matters most is precisely the one that aggregates from immaterial-per-instance into material-for-a-concentrated-subset. Operationally, the discipline records per-instance compression events with provenance and trajectory data (per *Initial-condition-plus-trajectory comparison*), then runs cluster-discovery across the accumulated records to detect concentration patterns that any single instance hid. The aggregation-horizon move connects the per-instance immaterial-to-both analysis to the framework's previously-scattered higher-order pattern findings — *Concentration risk* (trusted-talent-network typology), *Emergent-fractal* cohort-level dynamics (framework principle #17), distributional enforcement at the management-review layer (learning library design) — under one operation rather than disparate patterns. Anchors in Taleb's *ergodicity* distinction between ensemble-average and time-average (non-ergodic systems produce ensemble-positive metrics while individual time-series run to ruin; "no probability without ergodicity" is the discipline-level imperative), Geoffrey Rose's *prevention paradox* (1981, the distributional-shape-over-aggregate-metric move at population health scale), Pigou/Coase externalities as the foundational economic version, Griggs v. Duke Power (1971, the legal-procedural precedent that distributional-test finds harm individual-test hid), and Goodhart's Law distributional extension (optimization-pressure version). The AI psychosis case is the canonical illustration: per-session engagement-optimization is immaterial-to-both at the instance horizon (typical user has fine session) but aggregates to absorption-barrier concentration in a vulnerable subset (per *Non-ergodic compression*). Codified as Entry 36 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Observational discipline*, *Operator skepticism (uniformly applied)*, *Initial-condition-plus-trajectory comparison*, *Counterfactual generation*, *Non-ergodic compression*, *Immaterial-to-both*, *Concentration risk*, *Emergent-fractal*.

**Allocative skill cluster.** The competency set specific to Allocative-kingdom work: decision authority under ambiguity, delegation mechanics, span-of-control calibration, accountability separation from personal performance, and coalition-building across positions the role doesn't directly control. Teachable through structured curriculum and accumulated reps in the same way any technical specialty is — does not develop automatically through technical competence or tenure. The military's officer sourcing programs (OCS, ROTC) are the clearest institutional acknowledgment that this skill cluster requires deliberate development rather than being assumed to arrive with promotion. In Rigger, the Allocative skill cluster is a first-class development domain: the learning library should have an explicit Allocative concept cluster, the progression graph should require demonstrated Allocative reps as prerequisites for edges that increase Allocative demand, and the AllocativeSkillRecord entity tracks those reps separately from general learning portfolio entries. See also: *Kingdom*, *Hat-stacking*, *Progression graph*.

**Analog participation.** The off-platform version of the onboarding/mentorship process for new hires who opt out of the tool. Structurally equivalent — the role-definition discipline applies, the conversations happen — but without the persistent-record layer. Not a fallback or degraded experience.

**Archive manifest.** Small framework records-keeping discipline operationalizing the storage and discoverability surface for files migrated out of canonical project knowledge under `Mod-N retention horizon` (mod-N predecessors beyond the inline-retention horizon) and the handoff three-tier retention scheme (older chain-entry handoffs and pre-protocol handoffs). The discipline has three components: (a) **Storage tier** — files are migrated to GitHub-hosted archive repositories with per-file sensitivity classification: framework methodology (spines, public design docs) goes to a public repository under CC BY 4.0; operational records (handoffs, conversational records, stakeholder-specific records) goes to a private repository under all-rights-reserved. The two-repo split is operational discipline rather than a one-time choice — future archive operations apply the same per-file sensitivity classification. (b) **Discoverability tier** — a canonical `_archive_manifest.md` file in project knowledge serves as the index. Table-formatted by section (spines public; handoffs pre-protocol private; handoffs chain-entry private; expanded as needed for future archive operations), each entry records filename, archive date, byte count, SHA-256 hash captured at archive time, and raw GitHub URL. The manifest is the operational substrate for any future audit operation: future sessions navigating the framework can see what was archived, when, and to where, without having to enumerate the archive repos. (c) **Integrity tier** — the SHA-256 hash captured at archive time is the tamper-evidence surface. Audit operations verify byte-equality by computing `sha256sum` over content fetched from the raw URL (or from the user's local copy) and comparing against the manifest hash. A hash mismatch indicates either tamper or transcription drift since archive; the framework's chain audit dimension move provides the mechanism for recording any divergence found. First canonical instance is `_archive_manifest.md` produced in Entry 30's outcome (Recursive Updates Thread 1 operationalization of R1 + R5 from `architecture_pass_corpus_findings_20260510.md`); 23 archive entries at first instance (6 superseded spines public; 7 pre-protocol handoffs private; 10 older chain-entry handoffs private). Append discipline (codified in the manifest's own §5): manifest entries are write-once on archive; filename, hash, and URL are stable; if a file in the archive is replaced (e.g., correction) record both old and new with separate dated rows rather than overwriting. Codified as Entry 30 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Mod-N versioning* (the retention-horizon extension this discipline operationalizes), *Cross-session handshake protocol* (handoff three-tier retention this discipline operationalizes), *Structural recompression* (the architecture-pass discipline whose first reflexive worked example surfaced this discipline as a necessary operationalization).

**Asymmetry-explicit framing.** A compression strategy that names the loss function rather than the number. Example: "I'm rounding up where it matters because being 1% over is recoverable and being 1% under is a Council problem." Particularly useful with audiences whose loss function tilts differently from the speaker's. See also: *Compression strategy*, *Loss aversion*.

**Attestation status.** Field on artifacts (manager-walkthrough notes, AARs, mentor service records) distinguishing *signed* (collaboratively reviewed and confirmed by both parties), *self-reported* (created by one party without the other's confirmation), and *self-reported-confirmed* (created by one party, later confirmed by the other). Permits the framework to function before universal manager participation while preserving the visibility of the attestation difference.

**Bidirectionality.** The principle that development obligation flows in both directions, not just downward. A development system that captures only what staff did with growth opportunities produces evaluation pressure flowing one way; a system that captures both what staff did *and* what executives sponsored produces shared obligation. Operationalized in executive sponsorship cycles, manager-portfolio review at the City Manager level, and CM-callout-as-trust-relevant-event. See also: *Trust-relevant events*, *Survivorship bias*.

**Big Five / OCEAN.** A personality framework measuring five continuous trait dimensions: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism. The most empirically validated personality framework available, with decades of peer-reviewed replication. Conscientiousness is the strongest single predictor of job performance across contexts; Neuroticism (low emotional stability) predicts performance degradation under stress and elevated turnover risk; Openness predicts learning acquisition and performance in novel-problem roles; Extraversion predicts performance in leadership and high-social-contact roles; Agreeableness predicts performance in team-dependent and service contexts. In Rigger, Big Five data belongs exclusively in the private utility-function layer with the most restrictive consent defaults. It must not enter the role evaluation layer, must not be joinable with standing function performance records, and carries a schema-level `layer_assignment` field permanently set to `utility_function` to enforce this structurally rather than operationally. See also: *HEXACO*, *Utility function*, *Role versus utility function*, *IndividualCharacteristicRecord*.

**Buddy.** Peer-level onboarding role; same or adjacent department; daily check-in for week 1, tapering to as-needed. Day-to-day navigation function — printer location, PO process, who handles what. Distinct from the mentor (cross-departmental, portfolio demonstrator) and the coordinator (cross-cohort, synthesis function). No portfolio commitment, no AAR-eligibility by default; the value depends on the casual register that formal recordkeeping would erode.

**Bulk-upload-scramble class.** The mass-upload non-deterministic permutation mechanism observed empirically in the project knowledge upload pipeline: when a single bulk-upload operation includes files from more than one folder (e.g. select-all across the working directory and drop in one shot), some subset of the files arrive non-deterministically permuted relative to their source order, with the permutation pattern differing across repeated attempts of the same upload set. Distinct from *Tapeworm recursion* (the static-name caching mechanism that retains a prior version of a static-named file across uploads): tapeworm-recursion is a content-fidelity failure on individual static-named files within an upload batch; bulk-upload-scramble is a permutation failure on the upload batch's ordering or completeness irrespective of filename. Both fire at upload time but are diagnostically separable (a hash mismatch on a static-named file is tapeworm-recursion; a missing or out-of-position file with correct hash where it does land is bulk-upload-scramble). The mechanism's underlying cause remains undiagnosed — the name captures the observed pattern (mass uploads scrambling non-deterministically, evocative of how plaintext loses ordering through an unspecified pipeline stage) without claiming what the scrambling is keyed on. Per the diagnose-before-codify principle (#22), mechanism-agnostic responses remain available regardless of diagnostic state: **the folder-batch-granularity discipline** is the operational response. Upload files one folder at a time rather than bulk-selecting across folders. Folder-batch upload size empirically lands deterministically; bulk-all upload size scrambles non-deterministically. The discipline is mechanism-agnostic by design — it doesn't claim what the scrambling is keyed on (batch size threshold, cross-folder boundary, total payload size, file-count interaction effects) — it just names the upload-granularity boundary that structurally avoids the class regardless. Both-sides pressure-tested in Thread 2: folder-by-folder upload landed deterministically across multiple successive operations; bulk-all-at-once upload scrambled non-deterministically with the permutation changing between attempts of the same file set. The two-sided pressure-test is unusual for the framework's discipline architecture (most disciplines confirm via repeated same-direction instances; this one confirmed via comparison of the disciplined operation against the contrast operation), which is itself useful evidence — bulk-upload-scramble is real and folder-batch-granularity structurally bypasses it. Codified as Entry 34 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. The two-sided pressure-test counts as one observation-pair rather than two same-direction instances; the codification is at the framework's recurrence-threshold-equivalent for both-sides pressure-tests because the contrast establishes the mechanism's existence and the navigation move's effectiveness simultaneously. See also: *Tapeworm recursion* (distinguished failure mode at upload time), *Diagnose-before-codify* (the principle authorizing mechanism-agnostic navigation moves), *Mod-N versioning*, *Timestamped spine*, *Rename-as-navigation* (the per-file analog at the smaller granularity).

**Byte-clean base.** Small framework composition-time discipline: the stakeholder uploads a known-clean canonical-state copy of any fragile reference file to `/mnt/user-data/uploads/` at session start (or before composition of an entry that will modify that file), so Claude composes against verified bytes rather than reconstructing the file from project_knowledge_search chunks or conversation history. Avoids the failure class of reconstruction noise — small drift in structure, formatting, or phrasing that would otherwise compound across entries as Claude rebuilds from approximate sources. Three-instance pressure-tested at session boundaries before codification: Entry 21 first instance applying the discipline to `cohort_temporal_record.md` (per Entry 21 lesson #6); Entry 22 second instance same file (Entry 22 lesson #5); Entry 23 third instance same file (Entry 23 lesson #5). Mechanism-agnostic by design: the discipline doesn't claim what specific failure mode reconstruction noise produces (small wording drift, structural drift, missed sections, etc.); it just names the prep discipline that structurally avoids the class regardless. Distinct from Entry 24's timestamped-spine + mod-N-lateral versioning paired discipline (which addresses transmission-layer caching at upload-batch granularity) by addressing a different layer (composition-time reconstruction). The two disciplines are complementary: byte-clean base ensures Claude is composing from the right bytes; paired versioning ensures the produced bytes land canonically. Verify-then-redrop on the static-named README is the related composition-time + transmission-time discipline pair for the entry-point file specifically. Codified as Entry 25 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Tapeworm recursion*, *Mod-N versioning*, *Timestamped spine*, *Rename-as-navigation*.

**Case study.** Narrative writeup of how a situation was approached, shaped for a reader who wasn't there. Less raw than an exemplar — enough framing to make decisions legible. The natural form for City Manager all-hands callouts and Council recognition because they tell a story rather than dropping a thirty-page report on the audience. See also: *Exemplar*, *CM-callout surface*.

**Chain audit dimension move.** Small framework records-keeping discipline: a chain entry, during composition, audits prior chain entries (or chain state more broadly) for a dimension that the audit surfaces — precision overclaims in earlier outcomes, apply-checklist gaps, count drift, claim/record divergence, scope-precision gaps in prior operationalizations — and records the audit observation(s) as part of the *current* entry's outcome and skills fields rather than as a separate corrective entry. Chain integrity is preserved by the discipline's structural shape: the prior entries are not modified (their this_entry_hash values remain canonical), and the audit observation is itself part of the current entry's content covered by the current entry's this_entry_hash. The discipline operationalizes principle #23 (mechanical-verification-of-claims-catches-precision-overclaims) and operating constraint #10 (`Compare incoming claims to prior records before integrating them`) applied to the inbound-from-prior-sessions surface — the framework's own prior records are themselves intake that warrants comparison against current ground-truth before further integration. Each instance records a count of observations carried, parallel to how chain entries carry effort counts; an entry recording N observations is the Mth canonical instance carrying N observations. Five canonical instances at codification: Entry 25 first (one observation on Entries 23 and 24 pass-shape counting drift); Entry 29 second (one observation on Entries 27 and 28 count drift conflating cumulative pressure-test count with handoff-instance count, plus the implicit multi-thread accumulator framing); Entry 30 third with three observations (Entry 24 byte-prefix claim disconfirmed at corpus level; mod-N retention-horizon enumeration gap at the discipline-statement level; lightweight-handoff transcription drift surfaced by structural recompression); Entry 31 fourth (one observation on Entry 30 apply-checklist gap — the 23-file removal narrative authored from intent without grep-evidence enumeration on the apply-checklist surface); Entry 32 fifth with two observations (Thread 2 close handoff `handoff_20260511T030000Z.md` and the Thread 3 opener it produced both labeled the chain-audit cross-reference target as operating constraint #11 when the canonical Entry 16 forward-port placed `Compare incoming claims to prior records before integrating them` at constraint #10 with constraint #11 being `When records do not take a position, say so before continuing` — an off-by-one precision overclaim in the recommendation text caught at Entry 32 composition time and corrected in this glossary entry; Entry 31's R2 scope precision on `most-recent-two-mods` rolling-demotion — R2's catch-up scope was defined before Entry 31 produced mod7 and did not include `project_rigger_glossary_mod5.md` even though mod5 became mod(N-2) at mod7's production, leaving the most-recent-two-mods retention horizon at three inline glossary mods at Entry 31 close rather than two — remediated by Entry 32's apply-checklist enumerating mod5 alongside the natural-demotion mod6 under Entry 32's mod8 production). The discipline is rolling-pressure-tested through these five instances and is now structurally ripe for codification at glossary level (this entry); promotion-to-glossary was deferred at Entries 25, 29, 30, and 31 to avoid scope-creep on already-bundled entries per the diagnose-before-codify principle's mechanism-agnostic-navigation exemption. Codified as Entry 32 of `framework_decisions_record.md`; carry-forward of audit-observation count as a per-entry field for chain-audit-bearing entries is a subsequent refinement candidate at next pressure-test depth. See also: *Apply-checklist completeness* (the records-side mirror at the apply-surface), *Mechanical-verification-of-claims-catches-precision-overclaims* (the framework principle whose discipline this entry operationalizes against prior-chain records), *Chain-entry-text-as-separate-artifact pattern* (the composition discipline frequently bundled with chain-audit observations on multi-bundle entries).

**Chain-entry-text-as-separate-artifact pattern.** Small framework composition discipline: when a chain entry's substance is large enough that direct spine-block composition would carry composition risk (multi-file outputs with linked content, multiple bundle elements per Entry 23's bundling discipline, reflexive verification benefits from external readability before commit), the entry text is drafted as a standalone working artifact before being committed to the spine. The standalone artifact may or may not persist in canonical project knowledge as a working file after the chain entry commits — persistence is independent of the pattern; what matters is that the composition surface is externalized from the spine during drafting. Structurally distinct from out-of-chain artifacts (per Entry 28's `Out-of-chain session-close` codification) because the working artifact's text *does* become a chain entry: the artifact is the composition surface for the entry, not a standalone framework-level deliverable that sits alongside the chain. Pattern instantiated when at least one of (a) the entry will produce ≥3 NEW/REPLACE files with content linked across them (spine block + glossary mod + verify-script CONTENT_PATTERNS + handoff apply-checklist all share text fragments), (b) the entry is multi-bundle at L=5 effort per Entry 23's bundling discipline, (c) reflexive composition-time application of principle #23 benefits from the working artifact being readable end-to-end before file production. Three canonical instances at codification: Entry 30 architecture-pass session drafted the seven-codification bundle as `architecture_pass_corpus_findings_20260510.md` (this artifact persists in canonical project knowledge as the architecture pass's standing record); Entry 30 Thread 1 refined and committed the bundle using the prior artifact as composition surface (separate working draft did not persist); Entry 31 Thread 2 operationalized R2 + T043000Z archive operations + apply-checklist completeness codification using the same pattern (separate working draft did not persist; the spine block's substance was drafted externally before commit). At three canonical instances the pattern is at the framework's ~3-instance recurrence threshold for codification candidacy. The discipline complements byte-clean base (Entry 25 codification) at the composition-input surface — byte-clean base ensures Claude composes from verified-canonical input bytes; chain-entry-text-as-separate-artifact ensures the produced chain-entry text is itself reviewable before commit. Codified as Entry 32 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Bundling discipline* (Entry 23 codification; the recurring scope shape that motivates this pattern), *Byte-clean base* (the composition-input surface complement), *Out-of-chain session-close* (structurally distinct codification for artifacts that don't produce chain entries).

**Co-equal session limiters.** Small framework cadence discipline: each conversation thread (session) is governed by two co-equal limiters that fire whichever-comes-first within the thread, with both resetting at every new thread since the remedy in either case is a new thread. The two limiters: (a) the *effort accumulator*, capped at 15 units per thread under the existing 1/3/5 (S/M/L) effort-tagging discipline (per the `Cadence patterns at multiple scales` subsection of `framework_decisions_record.md` codified in Entry 15); (b) the *handoff-instance counter*, capped at N=1 — at most one chain-entry handoff per thread, since each chain entry produces one handoff and producing more than one within the same thread accumulates intra-thread drift in working context that the handoff protocol cannot cleanly transmit to a fresh session's working memory. The cross-session handshake protocol carries forward the in-progress work via the recommended-opener mechanism (component #4) — when a limiter fires, the new thread starts on a clean handoff and the work continues there. Out-of-chain session-closes (Entry 28's codified shape) do not advance the handoff-instance counter beyond zero, since they don't produce chain entries — out-of-chain sessions can be arbitrarily long without firing the handoff-instance limiter (the effort accumulator still applies if the session does enough non-chain work). The handoff-instance counter is conceptually distinct from the cumulative pressure-test count used for codification candidacy: cumulative pressure-test asks "how many times has this discipline been observed across all threads ever?" (informs codification at the ~3-instance recurrence threshold) and does not reset; handoff-instance asks "how many chain-entry handoffs has this thread produced?" (informs session closure at N=1) and resets per thread. Conflating the two produced count drift in Entries 27 and 28's claimed "fifteenth instance" / "sixteenth instance" of the filename convention (off-by-1 high in each case; recorded as chain audit dimension move's second canonical instance in Entry 29's outcome and skills fields). Under this discipline, the framework's "active codification arc" framing — multi-thread accumulator running cumulatively to 15/15 across multiple threads — is superseded; arcs are per-thread, and inter-thread codification work is opportunistic rather than arc-committed. Codification operationalizes the *per-entry-produced cap as co-equal cadence trigger* candidate carried since Entry 18's close handoff (`handoff_20260509T212404Z.md`) for ten subsequent entries (Entries 19–28), finally resolved in Entry 29 with the stakeholder's confirming direction. Codified as Entry 29 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Out-of-chain session-close*, *Handoff hash manifest*, *Timestamped filename convention*, *Cross-session handshake protocol*.

**Composition-time cross-reference verification.** Small framework records-keeping discipline: when a chain entry's text references numbered framework components — framework principles (`framework_principles.md` #1–#23), operating constraints (`core_principles_for_agent_behavior_mod1.md` constraints #1–#12), Cross-session handshake protocol sub-parts (README sub-parts 1(a)–1(d), 2(a)–2(b), 3(a)–3(b), 4, 5), or prior chain entry numbers — each numbered reference is verified against its canonical source at composition time before claim-finalization. Three sub-modes observed at three-instance recurrence threshold: (a) **point-precision-overclaim** sub-mode — single-reference off-by-one or wrong-number catch, corrected in the entry being composed (Entry 32 first instance: chain-audit-move codification originally cross-referenced operating constraint #11 when canonical numbering placed `Compare incoming claims to prior records before integrating them` at constraint #10; corrected to #10 in the Entry 32 glossary entry); (b) **bulk-impact-strategy** sub-mode — large-scale renumbering or restructure surfaces ambiguity at scale and the entry declares a forward strategy in its outcome field rather than rewriting every cross-reference (Entry 33 second instance: protocol restructure renumbered components, breaking 127 `component #N` references across 17 files; strategy declared as Update-live-spec + Leave-historical-records-as-composed + No-per-reference-chain-audit-notes); (c) **discipline-cross-reference-verification** sub-mode — codification text containing multiple numbered references is grep-verified against each cited canonical source before publication (Entry 34 third instance: hierarchical-principle-orientation + bulk-upload-scramble codifications reference principles #22 + #23, constraint #9, sub-parts 1(d) + 3(a), and prior chain entries 23/24/25/29/30/31/32/33; each verified at composition time before claim-finalization). The three sub-modes share the underlying discipline (verify numbered references against canonical sources before claim-finalization) but differ in operational response: point-precision corrects in-entry; bulk-impact declares a forward strategy in the outcome field; discipline-cross-reference verifies each numbered reference exists in the cited document with the cited number. The discipline operationalizes framework principle #23 (mechanical-verification-of-claims-catches-precision-overclaims) applied to the framework's own intra-document numbered-reference surface and operating constraint #9 (`Outcome fields are authored from grep evidence, not from intent`) applied to claim-finalization on numbered references; both source disciplines provide the general grep-evidence rule but neither names numbered-cross-reference verification as a specific surface. The Layer 2 content-pattern check in verify-script discipline (sub-part 3(b)) covers anchor-string verification after commit; composition-time cross-reference verification covers numbered-reference verification before commit, complementing the post-commit layer with a composition-time layer at the same precision-overclaim failure mode. Hierarchical placement is glossary-tier per hierarchical-principle-orientation: composers reference this entry when composing a codification that names other framework components by number; the discipline doesn't need to be in operating-memory during ordinary chain composition, only at composition time of codifications. Sibling discipline to *Chain audit dimension move*: both apply principle #23's mechanical-verification ethos to the framework's own records-surface, but chain-audit-move catches drift in prior chain entries (after-the-fact discovery), composition-time cross-reference verification catches drift before publication (before-the-fact prevention). Codified as Entry 35 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Chain audit dimension move* (the after-the-fact mirror), *Apply-checklist completeness* (the sibling composition-time discipline at the apply-surface), *Mechanical-verification-of-claims-catches-precision-overclaims* (the framework principle this discipline operationalizes against numbered-reference surface), *Diagnose-before-codify* (the three-instance threshold this codification crosses with three visible sub-modes).

**Compounding.** Percentage changes applied serially produce results different from the sum of those percentages. In multi-period contract analysis, the difference between simple and compounded figures can shift the political reading of a deal substantially. A three-year contract with 6% annual wage increases is 18% over the term simple but 19.1% compounded. **Fit drift.** The gradual divergence between what a role requires and what the person occupying it brings, as either the role or the person evolves. Most executive performance issues are fit drift surfaced too late rather than capability failures. Fit drift has at least two distinct forms. *Performance-against-role fit drift* is the gap between what the role requires and what the person currently brings — a person-performance question. *Kingdom-composition fit drift* is the gap between what the role's formal accountability describes and what its actual standing functions demand — a role-design question. The two can occur simultaneously but lead to different conversations and interventions. A system that cannot distinguish them will systematically misattribute organizational problems to individual inadequacy. The framework's value is partly in catching both forms early enough that the conversation can happen before either becomes a crisis. See also: *Kingdom-composition drift*, *Kingdom composition*.

**Goodhart's Law.** Generalized from the economist Charles Goodhart: when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Once people understand they are being evaluated against a proxy, they optimize for the proxy rather than the underlying thing. Standard remedies aim at preventing the optimization rather than reversing it after the fact: auditing, varying the measures, keeping outputs provisional, periodic review.

**Hat-stacking.** The condition in which a single role simultaneously carries standing functions from multiple kingdoms, which is the normal condition for most senior individual contributor and managerial roles rather than an exception. **Holland RIASEC.** A psychometrically validated framework organizing occupational environments and individual orientations into six types: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional. Person-environment congruence on Holland codes predicts tenure, satisfaction, and job performance, and the framework has one of the longest empirical records of any occupational fit instrument. Adjacent to the job typology taxonomy's Kingdom/Phylum structure — both are organizing work by what kind of engagement it requires — but designed for broad occupational fit across the labor market rather than position-level specificity within a single organization. **Initial-condition-plus-trajectory comparison.** Small framework records-keeping discipline (one of five operational components of *Observational discipline*): each observed data point is compared against (initial condition + actual interstitial events + decisions made within those) rather than treated as a snapshot to interpret in isolation. The trajectory is the substrate of meaning, not the moment. Operationalizes the path-dependence observation that current state = function(initial condition, environmental sequence, decisions); detection of drift requires the framework to record initial conditions for each observed subject, record interstitial events with provenance, and have a comparison frame against counterfactual trajectories (per *Counterfactual generation*). Anchors in Argyris's *ladder of inference* (1970, popularized by Senge 1990) at external observation scope rather than internal-conversation scope — the seven rungs (observable data → selected data → added meaning → assumptions → conclusions → beliefs → actions) and the reflexive loop (beliefs shape what data we select next time) become externally trackable through records rather than internally diagnosable through dyadic conversation alone. **Job typology taxonomy.** Diagnostic framework organizing work by relationship to value, structured analogously to biological taxonomy. Domain (Material/Symbolic — the substrate of work), Kingdom (Generative, Mediative, Interpretive, Protective, Allocative — how the work relates to value), Phylum (substrate the work acts on: physical goods, capital, information, people, systems), Class (primary instrument: measurement, narrative, force/authority, relationship, fabrication, procedure), then Order, Family, Genus, and Species down to job title. Functions as a meta-structure over Rigger's operational classification system: Rigger's function families describe what domain the work is in; the taxonomy describes what the work *does* to value and what it acts on. Most useful upstream of data capture — for interpreting fit drift and kingdom-composition drift when the operational layer surfaces them, for calibrating what development support is appropriate for which progression edges, and for understanding why cross-director friction often reflects kingdom-level differences in how people read shared priorities rather than personality conflict. See also: *Kingdom*, *Kingdom composition*, *Fit drift*, *Holland RIASEC*, *Requisite Organization*.

**Kingdom.** The primary level of the job typology taxonomy, defining how a role or standing function relates to value. Five kingdoms: *Generative* (creates new value where none existed — builders, engineers, planners, artists), *Mediative* (facilitates value exchange between parties — traders, coordinators, logistics, matchmakers), *Interpretive* (represents, translates, or renders value legible — analysts, accountants, educators, journalists), *Protective* (preserves value against degradation, loss, or threat — physicians, lawyers, inspectors, archivists), *Allocative* (directs value toward ends, determining who or what gets what — managers, executives, planners, commanders). Kingdom assignment belongs at the standing function level, not the position level, because most senior positions carry standing functions from multiple kingdoms simultaneously. See also: *Kingdom composition*, *Hat-stacking*, *Job typology taxonomy*.

**Kingdom composition.** The distribution of a position's standing functions across the five kingdoms, expressed as proportions. The position-fit cross-tab should surface kingdom-composition mismatches between actual portfolio (derived from standing function `primary_kingdom` and `kingdom_composition` tags) and nominal classification profile (the `ClassificationKingdomProfile` the classification was designed around). A position with substantially more Allocative demand than its classification profiles describes may indicate an under-classified role performing director-equivalent work; substantially less may indicate role erosion. Both are organizationally significant and neither is a simple performance question. See also: *Kingdom-composition drift*, *Position-fit cross-tab*, *Hat-stacking*.

**Kingdom-composition drift.** The gradual divergence between the kingdom composition the role's formal accountability describes and the kingdom composition of its actual standing functions. Distinct from performance-against-role fit drift, which is a person-performance question. Kingdom-composition drift is a role-design question — the position has evolved away from what its classification describes — and leads to different interventions. A director whose Allocative standing functions have steadily been absorbed by a deputy while they accumulate Interpretive and Generative work may be personally thriving while creating structural organizational risk: formal accountability hasn't moved with the actual work, and that gap surfaces badly when the deputy leaves or when Council asks a question the director no longer owns the answer to. The `PositionKingdomProfile` entity and the cross-tab extension track this over time. See also: *Fit drift*, *Kingdom composition*, *PositionKingdomProfile*.

**Structural recompression.** Small framework architectural discipline: when incremental compression has accumulated beyond what the existing structure can hold without becoming a compression failure of its own, replace the structure rather than continue patching. Distinct from the framework's existing compression machinery (compression strategy entries, asymmetry-explicit framing, layered presentation, range with a decision trigger — these are compression *moves* applied to specific content) by operating at a different scale: structural recompression replaces the structural substrate that holds the compressed content, when that substrate has itself become a compression failure. Sibling concept to rename-as-navigation and timestamped-spine + mod-N discipline (which operate at the per-file scale); structural recompression operates at the corpus or section scale. Distinguished from rename-as-navigation by scope: rename-as-navigation responds to a specific file's transit failure; structural recompression responds to corpus-scale compression-failure-of-the-substrate. Trigger conditions surfaced by the first reflexive worked example (the architecture pass on corpus, codified in Entry 30): operational trigger of ~60% project knowledge capacity; accumulation of incremental-compression artifacts (mod-N pyramids, timestamped spine retention) that no longer cash out — the substrate's compression mechanism produces dividends below the cost of the substrate's growth. Meta-property surfaced by the worked example: structural recompression at the corpus scale necessarily entails chain audit dimension moves on the codifications it supersedes — the act of replacing the substrate's compression mechanism exposes inconsistencies in the codifications that built up the substrate. The worked example bundled three audit observations (Entry 24 byte-prefix claim disconfirmed; Entry 24 mod-N retention rule lacks retention horizon; lightweight-handoff transcription-drift on chain head citation). Future structural-recompression instances should expect this bundling. Pressure-test depth at codification: one reflexive instance (this codification's own producing pass). Codification justified by operational need (give future architecture passes a referenceable concept) with explicit acknowledgment that further pressure-test will refine. **Tapeworm recursion.** The mass-upload static-name caching mechanism observed empirically in the project knowledge upload pipeline: when a batch of files is uploaded together and some files share names with prior uploads, some [0, n] of the static-named files retain their cached prior version rather than the freshly-dropped version. Single-file re-drops of affected files land canonically, suggesting the mechanism is keyed on batch-level deduplication or staleness behavior with respect to already-existing filenames rather than on file content or specific-filename fragility. The mechanism's underlying cause remains undiagnosed — the name captures the observed pattern (segmented uploads where some segments are stale prior versions, evocative of how tapeworm proglottids replicate with some segments being copies of an older one) without claiming what the cache is keyed on. Per the diagnose-before-codify principle (#22), mechanism-agnostic responses remain available regardless of diagnostic state: the framework's response is mod-N versioning of major lateral reference files plus timestamped-spine versioning of the chain entry file (see those entries). Detected via `verify_entry_N.py` hash mismatches at session pickup; remediable via single-file re-drop. Search-index-vs-filesystem divergence is a related observation surface — the search index appears to process new uploads independently of whatever filesystem-snapshot caching is happening, so the two views drift on exactly the files where the cache served stale content. Three-instance recurrence across Entries 22, 23, 24 openers (Entry 22 opener cohort_temporal_record.md; Entry 23 opener cohort_temporal_record.md again; Entry 24 opener project_rigger_glossary.md — the third instance on a previously-unfragile file isolated the mechanism via single-file re-drop landing clean, falsifying the file-specific filename-keyed hypothesis and unifying with the search-index-vs-filesystem divergence pattern as same mechanism viewed from two surfaces). Codified as Entry 24 of `framework_decisions_record.md`. See also: *Mod-N versioning*, *Timestamped spine*, *Diagnose-before-codify*, *Rename-as-navigation*.

**Thread opener protocol.** Small framework session-routine discipline naming the standard sequence at thread open: (1) **State verification** — read `README.md` pointer block (current canonical chain head, current canonical artifacts pointer block including chain spine + most-recent handoff); run the most recent `verify_entry_N.py` against canonical project knowledge state; confirm lateral file state matches the latest handoff's claims. (2) **Handoff read** — read the latest handoff file plus any predecessors its Recommended opener section explicitly points to. (3) **Mode elicitation** — ask the stakeholder which thread mode applies, in plain prose with canonical phrasing: *"Which mode for this thread? (a) Hypothesis — generate, test against existing data, identify gaps; (b) Build — work current build needs; (c) AAR — chain entry composition or in-flight updates (widget-driven, pending build)."* Mode-elicitation surface is plain prose for the current single-stakeholder project shape; tappable-options elicitation tool deferred per stakeholder direction. (4) **Mode execution** — proceed in the selected mode. **First-turn-obligation rule.** The protocol fires unconditionally on the first turn of every new thread regardless of the first prompt's content. If the first prompt is substantive (a question, a directive, an upload), the composer answers it first, then runs the opener routine before yielding the turn. If the first prompt is conversational ("hello", "hi"), the opener routine is the entire first response. The intent is that no thread proceeds to a second turn without state verification, handoff read, and mode selection on record. The discipline supplies the missing front-of-thread routine that prior threads opened ad hoc, producing variable opener shapes and occasionally missing the state-verification step entirely. Distinct from *Cross-session handshake protocol*: cross-session handshake governs file-pairing structure across thread boundaries (the (handoff_T, spine_T, lateral_T) triple, verify-script externalization, replacement-always); thread opener protocol governs the session-process routine at the moment a new thread starts — a session-process complement to the file-structure protocol rather than a sub-part of it. Mode (c)'s widget-driven operation depends on an AAR-entry widget that has not yet been designed or built; mode (c) sessions are not bookable until the widget exists. The widget build is itself mode-(b) work and a sequenced prerequisite for mode-(c) thread openers; carried forward as deferred build candidate per stakeholder direction. Hierarchical placement per *Hierarchical-principle-orientation*: protocol-component tier with authoritative codification surface at the new top-level "Thread opener protocol" section in `README.md` (after section 5 "Navigation-under-failure"); this glossary entry is the terminology-pointer back to the authoritative codification. Empirical confirmation evidence at codification: Thread 13 "hello" experiment (fresh thread opened in the project labeled "Greeting" with the single message "hello") produced a 0-second-thinking response of "Hi! How can I help you today?" with no proactive state verification, no handoff read, no mode elicitation — one-instance pressure-test confirmation that the protocol is necessary load-bearing structural surface rather than discretionary recommendation. Codification election per *Diagnose-before-codify*'s stakeholder-election mechanism (stakeholder-elected rather than recurrence-threshold-reached) given severe failure mode (continuity loss at every new thread) and clear diagnostic surface. Codified as Entry 40 of `framework_decisions_record.md` and as a new top-level protocol section in `README.md`. **Timestamped filename convention.** Small framework composition discipline: file types whose canonical durability is keyed on temporal ordering use a UTC ISO 8601 compact filename suffix `_YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.md`. Original baseline: handoff documents per Entry 18 codification (`handoff_YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.md`, established 2026-05-09 in `handoff_20260509T204202Z.md`). Extended to the chain entry file by Entry 24's timestamped-spine discipline (`framework_decisions_record_<TIMESTAMP>.md`), which uses the same convention to make the recursive (handoff_T, spine_T) pairing structurally well-defined: the timestamps key both files identically, so the pair is mechanically reconstructable at any future T from filename alone. Extended to lateral reference files by Entry 35's `Timestamped lateral versioning` discipline (`<filename_stem>_<TIMESTAMP>.md`, with TIMESTAMP being the entry-producing-timestamp), which makes the (handoff_T, spine_T, lateral_T) triple structurally well-defined: any lateral file's filename directly indexes its producing entry in the spine, since they share a timestamp. The convention's load-bearing properties: (a) lexicographic sort on filename equals chronological sort, since UTC ISO 8601 compact format is sort-stable; (b) every new instantiation produces a fresh filename, providing automatic tapeworm-recursion bypass per Entry 24 (no batch-upload cache key collision possible across timestamps); (c) the `Z` suffix encodes the timezone explicitly so PT/UTC drift across the framework's working sessions doesn't introduce ambiguity. The convention is what lets file types added to the chain framework (e.g. retrospective documents, audit notes, alongside-chain artifacts that warrant timestamp-keyed durability such as `single_loop_double_loop_translation_table_20260510T080000Z.md`, and now lateral reference files per Entry 35) adopt the same property without re-codification — name the convention once at glossary level, and any subsequent file type can apply it by following the suffix pattern. Sixteen-instance cumulative pressure-test through `handoff_20260510T120000Z.md` (Entry 28's close) at the time of Entry 29 codification (sixteen handoffs counting from `handoff_20260509T204202Z.md` plus this Entry 29 close handoff; cumulative count is independent of the per-thread handoff-instance counter codified alongside in `Co-equal session limiters` — see the cumulative-vs-per-thread distinction in that entry). Entry 35 lateral extension cumulative pressure-test continues from the spine + handoff cumulative count, since both pre-existing file types continue to use the convention identically. Codified as Entry 29 of `framework_decisions_record.md`; extended to lateral files as Entry 35. **Format cutover at Entry 40.** The UTC-Z suffix is superseded by PT-local-no-Z per *Wall-clock-derived timestamp discipline*. Subsequent filenames use `_YYYYMMDDTHHMMSS.md` with PT-local time as the invariant; the `Z` suffix's explicit-timezone-encoding property is replaced by the PT-local invariant being stated in the convention's discipline statement. Historical UTC-Z filenames remain as fossils per the chain's append-only construction. The format cutover is paired with the wall-clock-derived discipline (rather than codifying only the discipline and leaving the format mismatch in place) to remove the conversion surface between the filename time domain and the composed-at notation time domain — the same time-domain conversion overhead that contributed to the drift failure mode the wall-clock-derived discipline addresses. See also: *Timestamped spine* (Entry 24 application to the chain entry file), *Timestamped lateral versioning* (Entry 35 application to lateral reference files), *Recursive (handoff_T, spine_T) pairing* (Entry 24 baseline, depends on the convention being applied identically to both files), *Tapeworm recursion* (mechanism the fresh filenames bypass), *Co-equal session limiters* (cumulative-vs-per-thread distinction), *Cross-session handshake protocol* (sub-part 1(a) in `README.md` documents the convention's handoff application; sub-part 1(b) documents the spine application; sub-part 1(c) documents the lateral application post-Entry-35).

**Timestamped lateral versioning.** Small framework discipline: lateral reference files (`framework_principles.md`, `project_rigger_glossary.md`, `core_principles_for_agent_behavior.md`, `cohort_temporal_record.md`) are versioned with the UTC timestamp filename convention (per `Timestamped filename convention`), suffix pattern `_YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.md` with TIMESTAMP equal to the producing chain entry's timestamp. The first canonical instance is `project_rigger_glossary_20260512T160000Z.md` produced at Entry 35. Successor discipline to `Mod-N versioning` (Entry 24 codification): supersedes the integer-suffix dimension via Entry 35 forward-cutover after diagnose-before-codify analysis of five candidate cap mechanisms found that the mod-N integer's claimed semantic role (preserving sub-entry modification ordering when a single chain entry could modify a lateral file at multiple points) was empirically inactive — every observed mod increment in the canonical chain mapped 1:1 to a single chain entry's timestamp, so the timestamp pattern already used for the spine and handoffs absorbs the integer's actual role without loss. **Load-bearing properties.** (a) **Unified versioning model across spine + handoff + lateral.** One convention replaces three idioms (mod-N integer, timestamped, semantic-version). The triple (handoff_T, spine_T, lateral_T) shares the same timestamp domain, making cross-retrieval by timestamp trivial — any lateral file's filename directly indexes its producing entry in the spine. (b) **Cap question dissolved.** Timestamps are naturally bounded by calendar time; no cap mechanism to specify. The unbounded-monotonic-growth concern that the mod-N integer carried (mod9, mod10, mod11, ... approaching mod99) does not apply to the timestamp axis, which advances at the pace of chain entries that modify the file (~every-few-entries per file for currently-active laterals). (c) **Lazy instantiation transfers.** Files in the candidate set that are still at static-named (`framework_principles.md` lazy at pre-Entry-24 state) gain a timestamped suffix on first modification post-Entry-35 rather than mod1. The same trigger condition (first modification post-discipline-adoption) applies under the successor discipline; only the suffix format changes. (d) **Retention horizon transfers as most-recent-two-by-timestamp.** The Entry 30 `Mod-N retention horizon` extension (most-recent two mods inline; older mods archived) becomes most-recent-two-by-timestamp under the new convention: when a new timestamped lateral is produced, the third-most-recent timestamped version becomes archive-eligible. Rolling-demotion semantics are identical to the predecessor discipline; only the ordering axis changes (timestamp instead of integer). (e) **Historical fossils preserved.** Pre-Entry-35 mod-N filenames inside chain spines and older handoffs continue to reference the old filenames as historical fossils; chain integrity is preserved because the chain is append-only by hash construction and historical reference text is not rewritten. The transient three-mod-inline state from Entry 34 close (mod7 + mod8 + mod9) resolves at Entry 35 via the cutover apply-checklist enumerating all three as archive-bound. (f) **Cross-convention readability gains.** Reading a lateral filename in any context (canonical artifact pointer, handoff manifest, archive manifest, cross-references in other documents) now directly tells the reader when the file was produced (entry-producing-timestamp), which is operationally more useful than the integer's at-a-glance "Nth iteration" ordering signal. The integer's compression of "this is the Nth iteration" is lost; the timestamp's expansion of "this was produced on date D at time T" is gained. **Cutover apply.** Entry 35's apply-checklist archives `project_rigger_glossary_mod7.md`, `_mod8.md`, and `_mod9.md` to public archive `lateral_reference/` (three PUSH-TO-GITHUB + three REMOVE rows), resolving the Entry 34 transient three-mod-inline state in the same operation that codifies the cutover. Subsequent lateral modifications follow the new convention exclusively. Codified as Entry 35 of `framework_decisions_record.md` and as Cross-session handshake protocol sub-part 1(c) in `README.md` (the existing sub-part 1(c) Mod-N versioning paragraph is replaced with the new discipline statement). **Timestamped spine.** Small framework discipline: the chain entry file (historically `framework_decisions_record.md`) is versioned along its temporal axis as `framework_decisions_record_<TIMESTAMP>.md`, paired with the existing `handoff_<TIMESTAMP>.md` naming convention (UTC ISO 8601 compact format). Each chain entry produces a new timestamped spine file containing the full chain through that entry; older spine timestamps are byte-identical prefixes of newer ones since the chain is append-only by construction. The (handoff_T, spine_T) pair is the canonical durable unit at each timestamp T — handoff captures direction at T, spine captures application/state at T, and the pair is meaningful because each artifact references the other (handoff_T's chain-head pointer indexes into spine_T's last entry; spine_T's last entry's intent and why fields reference what handoff_(T-1) directed). The recursive pairing extends the chain-hash integrity property (each entry references predecessor by hash) across the directional axis as well as the state axis. Tapeworm resistance is automatic since every timestamp produces a fresh filename. Distinct from mod-N versioning (which versions lateral reference files along an integer axis); timestamped spine is for the chain entry file specifically because its temporal ordering is intrinsic and its pairing with handoffs is structural. The currently-static `framework_decisions_record.md` becomes orphaned at its current state at the discipline's first instantiation (Entry 24) and remains as historical fossil per the chain's append-only discipline. Recovery at any timestamp T from the (handoff_T, spine_T) pair is fully deterministic for chain state; full state recovery is strictly deterministic when the lateral-file mod manifest is included in handoff_T (per the handoff-as-state-pointer extension in Entry 24's discipline). **Wall-clock-derived timestamp discipline.** Small framework composition discipline operating on the *Timestamped filename convention*: any timestamp embedded in a filename or in a "composed at" notation inside a file is read fresh from system wall-clock at the moment of composition, never extrapolated from prior filenames, never carried forward from chain-entry text or handoff content, never assumed from conversational framing. The failure mode the discipline closes: filename and notation timestamps drift forward across threads when composers anchor on prior-file dates rather than wall-clock — observed across Threads 11–12 close handoffs whose composed-at notations and filenames advanced multiple PT-calendar-days ahead of actual production date (Thread 12 close handoff named `handoff_20260517T000000Z.md` with composed-at "2026-05-16 PT" against actual composition on 2026-05-12 PT, a four-to-five-day forward drift). **Whose utility function.** The framing question that runs beneath any compression decision. The same underlying figure can be presented multiple legitimate ways, and the choice of presentation is a value judgment about which audience is being served and which failure mode is being priced against. The skill is not "compress better"; it is *know whose loss function you're compressing toward, and surface that when audiences shift.*

**Working Genius / WIDGET.** Lencioni's model identifying six types of work required across a project lifecycle: Wonder (sensing gaps, asking generative questions), Invention (creating novel solutions), Discernment (evaluating with judgment), Galvanizing (rallying people toward action), Enablement (supporting and filling gaps), Tenacity (pushing through to completion). Each person has two Working Geniuses (energizing and natural), two Working Competencies (adequate), two Working Frustrations (draining). Maps roughly onto kingdoms: Wonder and Invention are early-phase Generative; Discernment maps to Interpretive; Galvanizing maps to Allocative; Enablement maps to Mediative and Protective; Tenacity is late-phase across kingdoms. In Rigger, useful as conversation scaffold for the utility function layer rather than as a classification instrument — the validation evidence does not support load-bearing use. Best accumulated from AAR substrate reflection prompts over time (which phases were most energizing, which required the most compensatory effort) rather than from a single self-report assessment. Complements Big Five: the Big Five describes stable behavioral tendencies that don't change much across contexts; Working Genius describes phase-level energization within a project. Together they give a more complete picture of utility function than either provides alone. See also: *Utility function*, *AAR substrate*, *Big Five / OCEAN*, *Kingdom*.
