# Cohort Temporal Record
**Status:** Working artifact, started 2026-05-07. Captures structured operational record of the seven-director cohort profile work and the framework-level findings produced along the way. Designed to be appended-to rather than rewritten.

**What this is:** A records-keeping instrument the framework's own principles call for. The profile work has been generating exactly the kind of trust-relevant event records, perspective-gap findings, and provenance-tagged assertions the framework architecture is built to capture; this artifact instantiates that discipline rather than continuing to let it live as conversational narrative.

**What this is not:** Authoritative on any individual fact. P-lens claims here are public-record-derived inferences with confidence variation; O-lens claims are sourced to Dan with disclosed bias; I-lens claims are inference and corrigible. The four-layer authority schema applies — position-record claims should be cross-checked against position control / Transparent California; behavioral notes are observed-or-inferred and flagged as such.

**How to use:** New events get appended to the Events Log with date, subject(s), provenance, and lens. New findings get added to the Framework Findings Log. People Index entries get updated as new lens content lands. Source Bias Registry tracks declared dispositions. Open Questions tracks what's outstanding and who can answer.
